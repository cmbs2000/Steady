# Steady — Content Inventory

Snapshot of everything currently seeded in the worksheet library, the reading library, and the links between them.

## 1. Worksheets (28)

| Title | Step/Category | Type | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| Daily Gratitude Check-In | Daily | check-in | A short daily practice to build gratitude and awareness of recovery-supporting behaviors. |
| Daily Trigger Log | Daily | check-in | Tracks daily triggers and cravings to spot patterns over time. |
| Evening Reflection | Daily | check-in | A short nightly wind-down to close out the day honestly before sleep. |
| HALT Check | Daily | check-in | A fast self-check using the classic HALT acronym to catch relapse risk early, any time of day. |
| Powerlessness & Unmanageability Inventory | Step 1 | worksheet | Establishes acceptance of powerlessness over addiction and the resulting unmanageability, the foundation for the rest of the program. |
| Unmanageability Timeline | Step 1 | worksheet | Builds a year-by-year timeline of consequences to reinforce acceptance of Step 1 through concrete evidence rather than abstract admission. |
| Coming to Believe | Step 2 | worksheet | Explores the sponsee's relationship with a Power greater than themselves and begins building hope that sanity can be restored. |
| Redefining a Higher Power | Step 2 | worksheet | Helps a sponsee who struggles with traditional religious concepts explore a personal, workable understanding of a Higher Power. |
| Letting Go of Control | Step 3 | worksheet | Identifies specific areas of life where self-will has taken over, to practice the daily Step 3 decision in concrete terms. |
| The Decision | Step 3 | worksheet | Works through the willingness to turn one's will and life over to the care of a Higher Power, on a daily and situational basis. |
| Relationship & Conduct Inventory | Step 4 | worksheet | Reviews past relationships and personal conduct, examining selfishness, dishonesty, and harm caused, per the traditional Step 4 relationship review. |
| Resentment, Fear & Harms Inventory | Step 4 | worksheet | The classic searching and fearless moral inventory — resentments, fears, and harms done to others, along with the sponsee's part in each. |
| Admission | Step 5 | worksheet | Prepares the sponsee to admit the exact nature of their wrongs to themselves, their Higher Power, and another person — usually their sponsor. |
| Preparing to Share Your Inventory | Step 5 | worksheet | Helps a sponsee organize and prepare emotionally before reading their Step 4 inventory aloud to another person. |
| Character Defects Deep Dive | Step 6 | worksheet | Goes defect-by-defect through the most common character defects to build specific, honest willingness rather than a vague general one. |
| Readiness | Step 6 | worksheet | Builds willingness to have character defects removed, distinguishing willingness from simply wanting the consequences of those defects to stop. |
| Humility | Step 7 | worksheet | Puts Step 6's readiness into action through a humble request that shortcomings be removed, rather than self-willed effort alone. |
| Practicing Humility Daily | Step 7 | worksheet | Turns the Step 7 request into daily, practical action rather than a one-time prayer. |
| The Amends List | Step 8 | worksheet | Builds a complete list of everyone harmed by the sponsee's addiction, and works toward willingness to make amends to all of them. |
| Understanding Harm Before Listing It | Step 8 | worksheet | Helps a sponsee distinguish real harm from guilt or resentment before finalizing their Step 8 list. |
| Amends Readiness Check | Step 9 | worksheet | A gut-check worksheet to confirm an amends will not cause further harm before it is made. |
| Making Amends | Step 9 | worksheet | Plans direct amends to those on the Step 8 list, wherever possible, without causing further injury. |
| Daily Inventory | Step 10 | worksheet | Establishes the ongoing practice of daily self-examination and prompt admission of wrongs, keeping the moral inventory current. |
| Spot-Check Inventory | Step 10 | worksheet | A quick, in-the-moment inventory to use during the day, not just at night, when strong emotion first appears. |
| Building a Practice | Step 11 | worksheet | Helps a sponsee design a realistic, sustainable prayer and meditation routine instead of an idealized one they won't keep. |
| Prayer & Meditation | Step 11 | worksheet | Deepens conscious contact with a Higher Power through prayer and meditation, seeking guidance rather than control over outcomes. |
| Carrying the Message | Step 12 | worksheet | Reflects on the spiritual awakening that has occurred through the steps and how the sponsee can now carry this message to others and practice these principles daily. |
| Living the Principles | Step 12 | worksheet | Examines how well the sponsee is applying recovery principles outside of meetings — at work, home, and in relationships. |
